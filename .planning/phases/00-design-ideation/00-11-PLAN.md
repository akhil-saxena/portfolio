<objective>
Put the public charcoal resolution and the two case-study templates in front of Akhil, get
the three derived judgements confirmed or overridden, and write the outcome down.

Purpose: VALIDATION.md classifies exactly two things in this phase as manual-only for good
reason. The ivory grep proves the ABSENCE of ivory values, not the PRESENCE of a good result —
"Work and Photos read as charcoal, not recoloured ivory" is a design judgement and no script
can make it. Likewise "both templates render against real drafted copy, not lorem" is
judgeable only by looking. UI-SPEC additionally flags three derived items whose defensibility
was asserted rather than reviewed, and says explicitly that they will proceed unless
overridden — this is where that override window actually opens.

Output: two recorded sign-offs and three confirm-or-override answers, appended to the public
design notes; any new gap filed in `00-FINDINGS.md`.
</objective>

<structural_note>
This plan is a review gate. It contains NO implementation and creates no sketch — plans 09 and
10 built everything being reviewed. Its only writes are recorded outcomes in two committed
Markdown files.

The sketches are served locally from the gitignored playground. Start them with
`cd .playground && npx astro dev`, which does NOT touch `dist/` and therefore does not
interfere with any built artefact.

Automatic advancement is disabled for this plan. `workflow.auto_advance` is `false` in
`.planning/config.json`, and even were it true, a design-judgement gate is not
auto-approvable — the entire reason these two verifications are manual is that no automated
signal substitutes for them.
</structural_note>
