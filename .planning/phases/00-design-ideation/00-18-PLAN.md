template assuming one spelling **drops a whole section from three of five studies and still
renders a page**.

what the compression cost each study.

Requirements on the script, each of which exists because of a specific hazard already hit in

  error naming the file, both accepted spellings, and the headings actually found.
- **Count words the same way `check-copy-length.mjs` does** — strip markdown furniture, strip
  HTML comments before counting (drafting comments are meta about the study, not the study),
  and treat anything containing a letter or digit as a word. Import or duplicate that file's
  `countWords` and its comment-stripping so the two scripts cannot disagree; if you duplicate,
  say in a comment why (a `.planning/` script must run with no install step and there is no
  module boundary here).
- **Strip `[source: ...]` markers from the count but assert their survival separately.** They
  are provenance furniture, not prose; counting them would let a compression pass hit the band
  by keeping citations and cutting sentences.
- **Enforce the band on exactly the four slugs this plan owns** — `cairn`, `hued`, `momentum`,
  `timeshift` — in a named `OWNED` array, and **report but do not fail on** `design-system`.
  Comment the reason at the array: `case-design-system.md` is being compressed by a parallel
  run to the same 500-700 target and this plan must not edit it. State the condition under
  which the exclusion ends: plan 00-20's build-time loader assertion covers all five, so this
  script's exclusion is a scheduling artefact and not a permanent carve-out.

What compression means here, in order of what to cut first:

1. **Cut the drafting comments and the lead paragraph before `## Problem` down to nothing they
   do not earn.** Plan 10 measured a 1,325-word gap between the whole-file counts (7,011) and
   the four-section counts (5,686); that delta is leads and HTML comments. They do not count
   toward the band, so cutting them buys nothing against R-1 — cut them only where they are
   now wrong (every reference to "long form", "short form", "per D-39", and to the two
   templates is now false and must go).

3. **Preserve every `[source: ...]` marker on a claim you keep**, and delete the marker with
   the claim when you cut it. A marker orphaned from its claim is worse than no marker.
4. **Preserve all five `[NEEDS AKHIL]` blocks and keep each at 40+ words of prose.** D-40's
   floor is a rule about handing the build phase a realistic length, and it survives the
   compression unchanged — `check-copy-length.mjs` enforces it and must still pass. Compressing

   target.
5. **Do not invent facts to fill.** hued and TimeShift are already near the band's top; they
   need a trim, not a rewrite. If a study lands under 500 after honest cutting, restore
   material rather than fabricate.

`badge:` frontmatter. The three non-final-content treatments from D-40 are unchanged.
