<objective>
Collapse the two case-study tiers into one and give each of the five studies its own route at
`/work/{id}`.

Purpose: the user said "one page per case, not like scroll to 10 cases in single page
scrolling down". `00-RESPONSIVE-CONTRACT.md` §7 reads that as **routing, not length** —
cases may still scroll internally — supersedes D-39, and R-3 resolves the route shape as
`/work/{id}` using the D-38 ids, because a case study is evidence *under* Work and this makes
the Work page the index of its own children. Plan 10's two stacked routes `/case/long` and
`/case/short` are what the direction rules out.

Most of plan 10 survives: the `copy.mjs` loader, the 68ch measure, `case.css`, the gap-block
`--wire` rule, the D-41 asset plan and the three non-final-content treatments. What does not
survive is the two-tier split and the two stacked routes.

Output: five case routes, one template, a loader that cannot silently drop a section, and the
one width breakpoint in the playground moved onto a class boundary.
</objective>
