<objective>
Move the reviewed photo content out of the Phase 0 brief and into the manifest, and backfill the
per-category order D-22 requires.

Purpose: `00-PHOTO-CONTENT.md` records that **all 39 `alt` values are filled, written by looking at
every photograph, and reviewed with Akhil on 2026-08-23** — twelve landmarks named, three species
identifications confirmed, both portraits deliberately left unnamed, and `place` filled on **16 of
39** *"only where the location adds something."* Its closing line is *"The `alt` column is complete
and reviewed. It is no longer blocking Phase 5."*

It is still blocking, in one specific sense: **that content lives in a markdown table and not in
`data/portfolio_images.json`**, where `alt` is present on **0 of 39** records. 00-ADMIN-IA §6 makes
`alt` *required for every published photo*, and the brief explains why in terms this phase cannot
soften — the public gallery ships zero framework JS, so *"`alt` is delivered on the `<img>` element
itself and is the entire non-visual experience of 39 images. It is not the fallback; it is the whole
channel."* A schema (03-06) that requires `alt` against a manifest that has none fails the build on
day one. So the merge happens here, before the schema, and the schema then holds the line.

The per-category order is the other half. D-22: *"one ordering cannot serve both views"* — the 39
photos are unevenly distributed (architecture 14, nature 8, wildlife 5, abstract 4, street 4,
portraits 2, product 2), so a global order that reads well end-to-end scatters each category's best
frames arbitrarily inside its own filtered view. The field is required before the gallery is built
against it, for the same rework argument that put CONT-04 in wave 1.

Output: 39 alt values, 16 place values, 39 per-category orders, a `focalPoint` disposition, and a
merge that is proven to actually read the brief rather than to have been typed out by hand.
</objective>

**Two traps in that file, both documented in it, both of which will bite a careless merge:**

1. Its opening HTML comment records an *ordering constraint*: the first literal occurrence of the

   contains either marker literally.
2. Alt values contain **commas, em dashes, apostrophes and the pipe-free but comma-heavy prose of

   of them.

information a listener can use. The merge must treat a marker as *absent*, and must **refuse** if a
marker appears in the required column.

**`description` and `tags` are out of scope for the merge.** All 39 description cells are still
markers, so there is nothing to merge; `tags` is contested (**OD-3**, settled in 03-06) and is
untouched here either way.
