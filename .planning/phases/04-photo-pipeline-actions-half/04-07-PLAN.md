<objective>
Turn one uploaded image into the five things a manifest record needs: four WebP variants, a
watermark on each, a 40-pixel base64 LQIP, the **source** dimensions, and the six mapped EXIF
fields.

The imaging logic here is the one part of the legacy pipeline that was right. It is re-implemented
rather than ported because `scripts/process-images.js` also produced the record, and that half
fails the Phase 3 schema in four independent ways. Read the legacy source for the spec, take the
spec, leave the record producer behind (it is 04-05's).

**One documented claim about it is false and must not be carried forward.** `CLAUDE.md`'s
Architecture section says "apply watermark to original and medium (not thumb)". The comment
contradicts its own code: `addWatermark()` is called inside the `for (const variant of VARIANTS)`
loop, so it is applied to **all four**. Only the 40px thumb and the `private/*-clean.webp` copy
skip it. Implement the code's behaviour, and say in the module header that the prose was wrong.

Purpose: PIPE-01 and criterion 1 — "resized variants in R2 … with EXIF read from the original".
Output: one derivation module and two unit suites that assert on **decoded output**, not on the
arguments passed to sharp.
</objective>
