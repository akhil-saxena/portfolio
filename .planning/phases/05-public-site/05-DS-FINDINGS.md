# Phase 5 — every design-system finding, in one place

**`@akhil-saxena/design-system@2.0.0-beta.2` IS NOW INSTALLED AND CONSUMED** (commit `2015b4d`).
The twenty-one findings below were measured against **`2.0.0-beta.1`**, which is what Phase 5 was
built on; **five of them are fixed in beta.2** and are marked ✅ **RESOLVED IN beta.2** in place,
with their original measurement kept. The other fifteen stand, and D-9 remains a retired precaution
rather than a gap.

| resolved in beta.2 | before | after (re-measured here, 2026-08-29) |
|---|---|---|

| **D-21** AppBar overflows at 344px | 14px on all six routes, both pointers | **0** on all six routes, both pointers |

**NONE OF THESE EVER BLOCKED PHASE 5.** Every one of them shipped as-is. That is the Core Value
working rather than failing: *"if a tradeoff arises between shipping something bespoke and shipping
it out of the design system, the design system wins, and any gap it exposes is a finding rather than
a workaround."* Twenty-one gaps, twenty-one findings, zero `!important`, zero re-implemented
components, zero local forks — and then five of them fixed upstream and consumed by version number,
which is the disposition paying off.

**D-21 was added by 05-15's six-class browser audit** and was the only one of the twenty-one VISIBLE
on the shipped site at a device class in the approved matrix. It was never a blocker — 14px of
horizontal scroll at 344px — but it was the one a reviewer would have seen. It is now zero.

Read out of the plan summaries `05-06` … `05-15` and re-verified against the installed package
before being written down — every citation below was opened, not remembered. Paths are relative to
`node_modules/@akhil-saxena/design-system/` unless stated. **Line numbers in the original findings
are beta.1 line numbers and are left as measured**; where a rule moved in beta.2 the resolution
gives the new one.

## What beta.2 changed about that, and what it did not

**Five of the twenty-one closed, and none of them closed by anything this repository did.** Each was
fixed in `../design-system`, published to the registry, and consumed here by version number — the
mechanism the Core Value predicts. The receipt above is unchanged: still no `!important`, still no
fork, still no re-implemented component, still no local copy of a design-system number.

**One line above is now a description of history rather than of a constraint.** `public-shell.css`
still declines to restate `--ds-appbar-h`, and it still should — but the reason has changed from
*"the property is unreadable from a sibling"* to *"the property is now readable from a sibling and a
consumer restating it would be a stale copy of a number that is correct at source"*. `home.css`'s
`--hm-bar-allowance` stays for the second reason too: at 344 and 390 on a fine pointer the bar still
paints 67 against a declared 57 (see D-2), so an over-estimate is still the right kind of wrong, and
the 72px allowance still clears every painted value measured. **Both comments were rewritten in the
same commit as this file** — a source comment asserting a fixed defect is exactly the kind of
citation this phase has spent itself hunting.

**Fifteen stand**, and the two most consequential for a reader are unchanged: **D-4** (`Link`
inline-sets its colours, so the footer's underline is black at 25% on a `#0d0d0f` surface — still
confirmed in Chromium at all six classes) and **D-13** (`TONE_STEP` collides, so all five
`StatusPill`s on `/work` render `data-step="1"` and the non-colour signal distinguishes nothing).
Neither was in beta.2's scope.
